### Do I need a licence to sell fish through AQUA AI?

If you sell fish or other animals as pets **as a business**, the law requires a licence. This applies even when you sell from home or online.

If you're a hobbyist selling a small number of your own fish's offspring, you usually don't need one. Selling often, or in larger numbers, can count as a business. If you're unsure, ask your licensing authority (listed below).

On AQUA AI you can list your stock and sell for collection in person without a licence. To offer **delivery**, upload your licence in the **Aqua Providers** app, under your account settings. We'll review it and switch delivery on once it's approved.

### How do I get a licence?

You apply where your business is based:

- **England.** Apply to your local council for a licence to sell animals as pets. The council inspects your premises first. Licences last 1, 2 or 3 years.
  GOV.UK: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-animals-as-pets-licence-england
- **Wales.** Apply to your local council for a licence to sell animals as pets.
  Animal Licensing Wales: https://animallicensing.gov.wales/en/news/44/are-you-selling-pets-you-might-need-a-licence/
- **Scotland.** Apply to your local council for a pet selling licence.
  Scottish Government guidance: https://www.gov.scot/publications/animal-welfare-licensing-activities-involving-animals-scotland-regulations-2021-guidance-pet-sellers/
- **Northern Ireland.** Apply to DAERA (the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs) for a pet shop licence. DAERA inspects the premises, and the licence is renewed every year.
  GOV.UK: https://www.gov.uk/pet-shop-licence-northern-ireland

Fees, forms and timings are set locally, so check your council's or DAERA's website.

### What do I upload?

Your current licence, showing:
- the licence number;
- the issuing authority;
- the issue date;
- the expiry date, where one is shown.

We remind you before it expires. If your certificate doesn't show an expiry date, we ask for a renewed one every two years.
